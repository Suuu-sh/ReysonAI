## Rake and SB limp (2026-09-24 ユーザー決定)
- Environment: online low stakes, 5% rake, 3BB cap, No flop no drop. Apply `rake(pot) = min(pot × 0.05, 3BB)` and `raked(pot) = pot − rake(pot)` to showdowns and all-ins that see a board; do not rake fold-only outcomes.
- Break-even calls that see a flop/board use `cost / raked(total pot after call)`. Steal and fold-equity auto-profit thresholds remain rake-free because those branches end preflop.
- SB RFI supports fold / limp to 1BB / raise to 3.5BB. BB 3bets the SB raise to 10.5BB and SB 4bets that 3bet to 24BB; other fixed 3bet/4bet sizes are unchanged. On the separate limp branch, BB may check or iso-raise to 3.5BB; SB may fold, call, or limp-reraise to 10.5BB. `limp-responses.json` is staged/generated after its source ranges and audited before publish. BB's value iso profile is based on a roughly 55%+ equity threshold versus SB's saved limp range, with limited blocker bluffs and playability-focused checks.
- The SB limp range is a separate branch from SB raise; do not reuse raise-response data as a limp response. Zero-limp hands in SB-vs-iso remain unreachable fold=100 placeholders, not recommendations.

## EQR / call EV (2026-09-24)
- Assumed EQR is centralized in `eqr.js` / `scripts/eqr.py`, with exhaustive cross-language parity tests. It is not solver output. IP means acting after every remaining opponent in SB, BB, UTG, HJ, CO, BTN order; three-way multiplies by 0.90; all-in uses 1.
- Call EV = equity × EQR × raked(pot after call) − incremental call cost. Every response / 3bet / 4bet / multiway / SB-vs-iso generator applies the shared policy: below −0.05bb no call; [−0.05, +0.05) at most 50%. Raise frequencies and opening benchmarks stay unchanged. Strength/nesting ceilings can further reduce calls; only positive-EV calls can be added minimally to retain the existing auto-profit gate.
- 2026-09-25: 3bet responses (three_bet type) fold 0% when call EV ≥ +0.50bb (non-4bet share → call; `THREE_BET_FILL_EV`). The high threshold is a margin for OOP realization in 3bet pots that the EQR table may overstate. Below it, authored baselines stand, except `PAIR_REVISIONS` in generate-three-bet-responses.py: a pair (EV ≥ +0.05bb) never folds more than a weaker-EV broadway call. Open responses keep `targetCall` (+0.10bb → 100%); 4bet responses are not filled. BB_vs_SB_limp mixes 30–35% checks into value isos (trap/protect the check range); HJ/CO vs UTG flat AA/KK/AKs 10% and add 5% light 3bets to EV-filled flats.
- 2026-09-25: `multiway-responses.json` holds 12 spots: the six BB spots, then `SB_vs_{opener}_{caller}call` for the same six (opener, caller) pairs (SB first response with BB behind; squeeze 13BB). SB calls 2BB into 8.5BB; its EQR is OOP × three-way 0.90 × `BB_BEHIND_EQR` (0.85, eqr.js / eqr.py; BB squeeze risk and four-way overcalls). SB profiles are squeeze-or-fold first with a small protected flat (AA/KK/AKs keep calls); SB flats far less than BB in the same history. Small pairs and suited connectors are −EV calls under this model and fold. The squeeze-width audit compares each hero with its own heads-up 3bet.
- `scripts/data/response-mixes.json` is the immutable authored open-response baseline, not the generated output or repeated legacy retention coefficients.
- `call-equities.json` stores 12,000-sample, per-spot/per-hand seeded equities and exact opponent-range / geometry inputs. The audit rejects missing or stale entries. `.local` caches are optional performance aids, never audit dependencies. `call-ev-report.json` records reductions, conservative consistency edits, positive-EV additions and >=+0.3bb uncalled hands.
- `negative-ev-call` is warn for call > 0 and EV < −0.05bb, error/publication blocker for call >= 10 and EV < −0.2bb. Borderline calls over 50% are also blocked. Existing balance advisory checks remain active and their spot lists are reported, not silently waived.
- Proven EV-capacity conflicts are explicitly reported as `ev-capacity-conflict` warnings: only when filling **every legal call** still fails the old auto-profit bound, and the saved strategy already reaches that maximum. This documents an unsolved equilibrium conflict; it is not evidence of GTO. Any avoidable overfold, missing/stale equity input, or negative-call violation still blocks publication. UTG-vs-HJ 3bet is the initial conflict (maximum 28.85% continuation versus 33.33% required), preserved for review rather than silently changing raises or the EQR assumptions.
