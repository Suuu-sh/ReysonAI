## 2. Scoped independent replay with complete stored-evidence integrity

The old `model11-selected-regression.mjs` is useful precedent, not a reusable acceptance contract for a new policy. Its manifest, source loader and comparison totals are explicitly hard-pinned to one historical 720,000-trial report, 4,999 selected trials and cache batch 512. Its `originalDealStream` correctly calls `samplePair`, `dealRunout` and the 24 action draws at every original index. Its selected producer executes only the selected strategy indices, writes original-index evidence, and compares complete returns, completion laws and full proof bodies. Its four cache-purity controls explicitly acknowledge that a sparse replay does not recover every original warm-cache history.

The current proposed 10,000-trial contract retains cache batch 64. Do not import the old 512-boundary or 4,999 totals. Selecting every first/last and every cache64 boundary would already select 314 trials per cell, or 22,608 across all 72 cells, before extra failure/completion cases. That is not required by the user's request for a subset of conditions.

A small new fixed-manifest adapter can use the existing sampling, completion producer, immutable evidence store and full-object comparison primitives while leaving the old contract unchanged. Suggested budget option, not yet selected:

1. Deterministically choose 12 cells, one per representative board, assigning each of the six profile × seat combinations twice. Bind the actual ordered cell IDs, independent selection seed, original report, policy pair, numerical source and cache64 identity. Add explicit source-bound known-failure conditions; do not choose favorable EV rows or reroll the selection.
2. For each selected condition, replay complete cache epochs. An initial/middle/final option selects epochs 0, 78 and 156: indices 0–63, 4,992–5,055 and 9,984–9,999. This is 144 trials per condition, or 1,728 base trials for 12 cells. The final epoch legitimately has only 16 trials. Add whole epochs containing specifically listed known failures, deduplicating overlaps. A budget requiring three full 64-trial epochs can instead use epoch155 and add the final16 explicitly.
3. Advance the original RNG stream through all 10,000 indices of each selected condition, including skipped strategy trials. Release at all original multiples of64, and execute every strategy trial consecutively within selected epochs. This reproduces the model-law cache history within each selected epoch. Process-global card-cache warmth and cumulative diagnostic counters differ; cold/warm/released controls must show that these do not change the compared numerical objects.
4. Separately scan all72 original cells and all720,000 stored rows. Verify exact cell identities/order, complete trial indices, chunk byte hashes and trial hashes, report and source bindings, status/count partitions, all EV aggregates/warnings, every referenced proof envelope and entire proof-body hash, and the exact no-missing/no-extra proof reference set. Use existing bounded reads and symlink/immutability guards. This is complete stored-evidence integrity, not a fresh numerical recalculation of unselected rows.
5. Keep the producer's existing native semantic proof/event validation once for all produced cells. The selected independent replay repeats numerical execution and semantic validation in its stated scope. A source-bound successful validation receipt may be checked for unchanged bytes afterward; hashes alone cannot replace the initial full semantic validation or establish policy quality. Keep selected replay counts and full stored-row counts separate in the resulting receipt.

The original producer currently validates a newly produced cell, then validates it again in the same call, and the full audit validates saved cells before generating and validating an entirely fresh report. An additive scoped audit should avoid automatically requesting that fresh720k operation. It must not weaken or relabel the existing full-audit entry point. The former selected adapter also performs native validation of the original report; copying it blindly would retain repeated semantic work the new scope is intended to avoid.

Required focused checks: deterministic exact selection, all12 boards and profile/seat balancing, known-failure inclusion and epoch deduplication, rejection of partial epochs except the final tail, variable-consumption RNG agreement through skipped intervals, original reset placement, complete selected-trial/law/proof equality, and rejection of changed/missing/reordered rows or proofs even outside the replay subset. Validate scope labels and ensure no selected mean is substituted for full720k quality metrics. No statistical equivalence or full independent-replay claim follows from this design.

Relevant existing sources: `model11-selected-regression.mjs` (`originalDealStream`, `produceSelectedCell`, `compareSelectedCell`, `runSelectedCacheControls`); `evaluate-model11-selected-regression.mjs` (`validateOriginal`, `selectedLane`, `compare`); `model11-completion-representative.mjs` (`produceCompletionRepresentativeCell`, native validation, full audit); `model11-completion-representative-store.mjs`; `model11-completion-representative-contract.mjs`; tests `postflop-model11-selected-regression.test.mjs` and `postflop-model11-completion-representative.test.mjs`.

## 3. Existing public-card caching and the smallest possible remaining gap

`defence.mjs` already shares card-only caches across Defence instances:

- `rankTable`, line118: all unblocked two-card combo scores and rank ordering for one final5-card board, keyed by an order-independent numeric board key; 3,000 entries, dropping300 oldest insertions at the bound.
- `tierArray`, line154: board-wide hand tiers, separate3,000-entry caches for flop/turn and river. River tiers reuse `rankTable` scores.
- `flopRunouts`, line185:300 deterministic runouts per flop, with their table list retained, bounded to16 flop entries.
- `finalTables`, line204: a river uses its rank table, a flop uses its cached runout list, and a turn rebuilds the48 ascending-river table-reference list on each call.
- Board texture caching and precomputed draw-owner masks already avoid other repeated card-feature work.

`range-equity.mjs` shares rank bounds through `WeakMap<table,…>`. It intentionally keeps weighted prefixes, per-card blocker lists and indexed plans on each range object. `releaseRangeTables` removes the dense indices/plans after exact values have been saved. `Defence` also stores per-context combo equities. Model11 primes each facing context in canonical combo order and forces the pinned JS arithmetic path. Do not pool equity results or these weighted plans by public board alone: posterior weights and the first-three-query arithmetic schedule matter.

The narrow remaining card-only candidate is `finalTables(turn)` list assembly. Reusing a tiny bounded private list could avoid repeated48 rank-table lookups and repeated array construction across contexts, while preserving the exact ascending river-card enumeration and the existing rank table objects. It would not remove genuine first-use evaluation. Any retained list also pins rank tables past normal eviction, so bound that additional retention tightly; avoid a second unbounded evaluator cache, and preserve the public array's mutation behavior rather than allowing caller writes to poison future lists.

The default recommendation is **no extra card-evaluation cache until a longer post-stage1 measurement demonstrates a gap**. For one fixed flop, there are only C(49,2)=1,176 possible final public boards, below the existing3,000 rank-table capacity. Cache64 release clears model/range graphs but does not clear this process-global rank cache. Thus a continuous10,000-trial cell can retain every final-board rank table it visits. The cold64 profile's13.2% inclusive rank-table cost is not evidence that the same fraction remains in a10,000-trial cell. Range-equity's10.8% inclusive cost is weighted numerical work, not proof of missing card-only memoization.

If the turn-list candidate remains measurable after stage1, focused checks should compare table order, score/sortedIds/sortedScores bytes, board permutations, distinct turn boards, mutation isolation, bounded retention/eviction, and release behavior. Then compare full current trial returns, laws and proofs under cold/warm/released operation with unchanged RNG, cache64 and canonical arithmetic. Existing `postflop-defence.test.mjs` tests cached tiers against independent hand/policy lookup; evaluator-oracle tests are already available. Do not rerun exhaustive2.6million-hand evaluator tests merely for an unchanged reference-list memo unless evidence indicates score computation changed.
