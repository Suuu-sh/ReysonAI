## Exact-zero deterministic paths versus actual observations

The new proof extension changes neither an action law nor its arithmetic/order. It adds `zeroLikelihoodProofVersion:1` to the model descriptor and versions the explicit execution/balance adapters as 2; simulation envelope version 2 retains the new diagnostic proof. Old reports cannot be reused under this identity.

At the first historical multiplication with positive actor support and exact all-zero after support, the core emits the typed `off-model-observed-action` with a frozen certificate. It identifies the earlier public prefix/actor/physical action, complete ascending actor support, each before weight, contemporaneous final physical mass and after weight, totals, model/source/artifact/belief/executor/order identity, and per-combo equity/fallback/sign provenance. Positive-factor underflow throws before this certificate is possible.

Only the mechanically enumerating balance facade may classify that event as `proved-model-unreachable`. It replays the earlier prefix from the same immutable source; reconstructs every positive actor-support combo; checks every final full-order/remainder/alias-pooled physical action law and exact zero product; and checks that the particular skipped request descends from the certified event on the same revealed board/history. A certificate from another board or branch cannot authorize a skip.

An explicit saved/capped fallback is a known declared law. Its exact zero can be proved while retaining unavailable-equity/unknown-sign provenance. An unknown status by itself is never proof of zero. Missing/invalid laws, omitted support, approximation, underflow, or an unrelated certificate cannot certify anything. The scope is the declared continuous policy law / own-realization model, not every finite-JavaScript-RNG boundary.

Actual-reference simulation does not use the gate-only classification. Its actual unsupported observations remain unresolved and prohibit cell EV and full representative completion, even if an exact-zero certificate is attached. Terminal reference actions retain the existing e51 behavior when no later candidate inference is required.

Board-level counts partition requested boards into evaluated, proved-base-unreachable and unresolved. Within visited unique effective decision prefixes, counts partition requested into evaluated, proved-model-unreachable and unresolved. Geometrically invalid paths and physical aliases remain handled exactly by the inherited kernel; prefix counts do not pretend to count every abstract history. Off-model stopping stages explicitly leave the rest of their requested stage incomplete.
